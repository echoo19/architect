# Research capture

`weight-space-programming.png` is the title block of "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming: LLMs can
natively read & write neural network weights" by Rikiya Takehi, Jayden Teoh, Alex Zhang and Omar
Khattab (MIT CSAIL), published November 2, 2026, at
https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/.

It was captured as the page is published, headless (chrome-headless-shell, 1280x900 viewport at
2x), on November 3, 2026, and only cropped to the title, authors, affiliations and date
(`runs/film/launch/wsp/capture.mjs`). Nothing in it was redrawn or edited. The launch film shows it
in its closing scene, which says Architect WSP was inspired by this research and credits the authors,
MIT CSAIL and the page's host on screen. No endorsement by the authors or MIT is implied.
