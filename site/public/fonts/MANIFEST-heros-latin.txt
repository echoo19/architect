Heros, Latin subset
===================

heros-regular-latin.woff2 and heros-bold-latin.woff2 are subsets of TeX Gyre Heros 2.004
(texgyreheros-regular.otf and texgyreheros-bold.otf), by Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki
of the GUST e-foundry, itself based on URW Nimbus Sans L.

The site loads them as a fallback for the marketing text on computers that have no Helvetica Neue
(Windows and most Linux systems). Where Helvetica Neue is installed, the page uses that instead.

What changed from the original files:

- Only the Latin letters, digits, punctuation, currency signs and arrows the page uses are kept.
- The outlines are unchanged. Hinting was dropped and the files were converted to WOFF2.
- No design change was made, and the glyphs are not edited.

The fonts are used under the GUST Font License, which is in GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt beside this
file. It asks that derived files be renamed and listed in a manifest like this one: the files here
are named heros-*-latin, and the page refers to the family as "Heros".
